Privacy Policy

StatStrike Tips · Effective September 17, 2026

StatStrike Tips ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and operated independently. This policy explains what happens, from a privacy standpoint, when you use the App.

1. No Account Required

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use it. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no personal data collected directly by the App from you.

2. Subscriptions and Payment

Paid tiers are sold and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Google, not us, collects and handles your payment information (card details, billing address, and similar) — we never see or store this information. Google's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's own Privacy Policy.

3. Information We Do Collect

Because the App communicates with our server to fetch fixtures, tips and FAQs, our server automatically logs basic technical information with each request, such as IP address, request timestamp, and the type of request made. This is standard for essentially any web server. These logs are used only for operating and troubleshooting the service, are not linked to any personal identity, and are retained only briefly.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing handles all subscription purchases, as described above.
  • football-data.org is the source of public football fixture data shown in the App. Our server fetches this data; your device never communicates with football-data.org directly, and no information about you is shared with them.

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools in this App.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect any in the first place.

6. Data Security

All communication between the App and our server is encrypted using HTTPS.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
